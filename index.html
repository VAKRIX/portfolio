About Me

I'm an aspiring Data Analyst currently studying at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Damietta University.

My approach focuses on practical business value—cleaning inconsistent records, analyzing trends, and building clear visualizations that highlight what the data is actually saying. During my Data Analyst internship at the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), I worked firsthand with messy datasets, applying data transformation techniques and developing interactive dashboards to support decision-making.

Whether it's uncovering revenue drivers in a telecom dataset or organizing sales KPIs into a clear dashboard, my goal is to make data accessible, understandable, and actionable for small businesses.
